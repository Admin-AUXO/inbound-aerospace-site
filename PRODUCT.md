## Product Purpose

Inbound Aerospace is building India's first private autonomous, reusable spacecraft: an uncrewed, wingless lifting-body spaceplane that flies experiments in low Earth orbit (350 to 450 km) for weeks of disturbance-free microgravity, then glides back at low g and lands on a runway so the payload is recovered at once. Success for the site: a qualified payload or partnership enquiry, an investor conversation, or a strong job applicant.

## Positioning

The return is the product. Capsules splash down at 5G or more, in the ocean, far from the lab. Inbound's lifting body re-enters at up to 2G (company claim) and lands on a runway, so sensitive samples come home gently and fast. Autonomous, uncrewed flight gives undisturbed microgravity that crewed stations cannot. The ISS retires in 2030, and launch costs keep falling.

## Operating Context

- Incubated at IIT Madras Incubation Cell, IITM Research Park, Chennai. Pre-incubated at Nirmaan (IITM).
- Mission profile in five steps: payload integration, launch on a commercial rocket, weeks in LEO, low-g re-entry, runway landing and recovery.
- Test programme in public press: 1 km drop test done; 3, 8 and 30 km trials planned; landing accuracy target 100 m; first re-entry mission targeted for 2028 (press says Q1 or first half).
- Near-term hardware: steerable parachute landing system, also sold as PADS for defence.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed by the company site: autonomous, reusable, lifting body, up to 2G re-entry, runway landing, weeks in orbit, 350 to 450 km altitude.
- Not public, must not be invented: payload mass, volume, power, temperature range, price per kg, launch partner contracts, mission dates beyond press reports, vehicle name.
- Press, not yet company-confirmed: pre-seed of more than $1M led by Speciale Invest with Piper Serica (Jul 2025); S-Booster 2025 finalist; Swavlamban 2026 exhibitor; ₹1 billion Chennai facility MoU; Space Angel retrieval agreement; iDEX recognition for the parachute.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the return: low g, runway, samples in hand.
2. Show the engineering, do not claim it. Facts, numbers the company has published, and the test ladder.
3. Every audience gets one clear next step: fly a payload, talk to us about investment, join the team, PADS enquiries.
4. Never state what Inbound has not published.
